Fennec — character mockup

A cartoon of the plush, drawn as parametric SVG so every pose comes out of one rig. Nothing here is a PNG: the pose sheet, the animation and the playable scene are the same block of geometry with different joint angles.

Pose sheet

Animation

The run is a four frame cycle: legs counter-swing, the body bobs two units, the ears lag behind the head and the tail swishes on a slower beat than the legs. The ear lag is most of what sells it as a fennec rather than a dog.

In a side scroller

Click the scene first, then arrow keys or A/D to run, Space or Up to jump, Down to crouch. The coins are there to show scale against the sprite.
