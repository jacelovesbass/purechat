TIC TAK TOE BOOM!

Four in a row. Two players, one phone. Three bombs each.

Computerplays amber
How it works
  • Tap a column to drop your token. It falls to the lowest free slot. Four in a row wins, any direction.
  • You get three bombs. Tap BOOM, then tap any token on the board. That token and the four touching it, up, down and either side, are destroyed. Corners survive, your own tokens do not.
  • A bomb costs your whole turn, and everything above the hole falls into it.
  • Playing the computer? It takes a win when it has one, blocks yours, and will not bomb unless the rubble leaves it ahead. It gets three bombs like you do.
  • A four made by the collapse still counts, even if it is your opponent's. If the dust settles and you both have four, the longest line wins. Equal longest is a dead heat.

Posting closes the game and drops the result into the conversation.
